Chilika Lake Tour Package from Bhubaneswar

Explore Asia's largest brackish water lagoon. Spot rare playful Irrawaddy dolphins at Satapada, visit the sacred Kalijai Island Temple, and feast on world-famous fresh Chilika crabs and prawns.

Home / Tour Packages / Chilika Lake Tour

Asia's Greatest Coastal Wetland & Wildlife Haven

Spanning over 1,100 square kilometers, Chilika Lake is a paradise designated under the Ramsar Convention. Whether embarking on a boat ride to spot Irrawaddy dolphins at Satapada or taking a scenic cruise from Barkul to Kalijai Island, Biswa's Tour and Travels provides dedicated round-trip cab packages from Bhubaneswar with verified OTDC boat booking guidance.

Chilika Tour Highlights

1. Satapada Irrawaddy Dolphin Point

Take a motorized boat safari into the lagoon where Irrawaddy dolphins surface playfully. Cruise ahead to the Sea Mouth where Chilika lagoon meets the Bay of Bengal.

2. Kalijai Island Temple (Barkul Route)

Sail across the tranquil blue waters from Barkul or Balugaon to visit Maa Kalijai Temple situated on a serene rocky island inside the lake.

3. Mangalajodi Bird Paradise (Winter Season)

From November to February, witness over a million migratory birds nesting in marshlands. Silent wooden boat tours guided by former poachers-turned-conservationists.
