Daringbadi Tour Package from Bhubaneswar

Escape to the serene "Kashmir of Odisha" perched 3,000 feet above sea level in Kandhamal district. Crisp mountain air, lush coffee plantations, whispering pine forests, and tumbling cascades.

Home / Tour Packages / Daringbadi Tour

Breathe the Pine-Scented Air in Daringbadi

Situated in the tribal heartland of Kandhamal, Daringbadi is the only place in Odisha that experiences sub-zero winter temperatures with frost. With Biswa's Tour and Travels, you get robust, hill-tested SUVs driven by experienced mountain chauffeurs who know every winding curve and scenic lookout point.

3 Days / 2 Nights Itinerary Plan

Day 1: Bhubaneswar ➔ Ghat Roads ➔ Daringbadi

Early departure from Bhubaneswar via Nayagarh and Phulbani ghats (~250 KM, 6 hrs). Arrive in Daringbadi and check in to your eco-resort. Enjoy sunset at Hill View Park and Butterfly Park.

Day 2: Waterfalls, Coffee Gardens & Silent Valley

Visit Midubanda Waterfall, Dasingbadi Pine Forest, Coffee & Black Pepper Gardens, Lovers Point, and the breathtaking Mandasaru Gorge (Silent Valley of Odisha).

Day 3: Belghar Nature Camp ➔ Return to Bhubaneswar

Morning visit to Emu Bird Sanctuary. Pack local organic turmeric and spices, and start the comfortable descent back to Bhubaneswar.
