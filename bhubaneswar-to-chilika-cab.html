Which Route to Choose?

- Satapada Route (110 KM): Best for spotting rare Irrawaddy dolphins and visiting the Sea Mouth (lagoon opening into the Bay of Bengal).
- Barkul / Balugaon Route (105 KM via NH-16): Best for visiting Maa Kalijai Island Temple, staying at OTDC Panthanivas, and scenic sunset cruises across broad waters.
