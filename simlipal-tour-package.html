Simlipal National Park Tour Package

Journey deep into the wilderness of Mayurbhanj district. Encounter Royal Bengal Tigers, Asian Elephants, and witness India's second-highest waterfall, Barehipani, tumbling down red cliff faces.

Home / Tour Packages / Simlipal National Park

Untamed Wilderness & Majestic Waterfalls

Encompassing nearly 2,750 square kilometers of dense sal forests, Simlipal is a UNESCO World Network of Biosphere Reserves. With Biswa's Tour and Travels, we provide high-clearance SUV transport from Bhubaneswar right up to the forest gates at Pithabata / Jashipur, assisting you with forest department permits and eco-cottage logistics.

Simlipal Safari Highlights

1. Barehipani & Joranda Waterfalls

Stand in awe before Barehipani Falls (399 meters, two-tiered) and the misty Joranda Falls dropping 150 meters into deep wooded gorges.

2. Jungle Safari & Wildlife Sighting

Track signs of Bengal tigers, leopards, wild elephants, sambar deer, barking deer, and over 300 bird species including the hill myna.

3. Eco-Tourism Cottage Stays

Spend nights at Jamuani, Chahala, or Ramtirtha nature camps operated by the forest department, surrounded by tranquil jungle sounds.
