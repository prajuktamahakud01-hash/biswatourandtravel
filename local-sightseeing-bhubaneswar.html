Top Sightseeing Highlights in Bhubaneswar

Lingaraj Temple

The magnificent 11th-century Kalinga architecture masterpiece dedicated to Lord Harihara (Shiva & Vishnu).

Dhauli Shanti Stupa

Site of the historic Kalinga War where Emperor Ashoka embraced Buddhism and non-violence.

Khandagiri & Udayagiri

Ancient 1st-century BCE rock-cut Jain monastic caves showcasing elaborate inscriptions of King Kharavela.

Nandankanan Zoo

World-renowned biological park home to white tigers, lion safaris, botanical garden & ropeway.
