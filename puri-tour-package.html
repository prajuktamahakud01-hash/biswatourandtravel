Spiritual & Scenic Puri Pilgrimage Experience

Puri is one of the revered Char Dham pilgrimage sites in India. Our customized Puri holiday and darshan packages from Bhubaneswar are designed to offer maximum peace of mind, eliminating parking bottlenecks and long queues so you can focus entirely on divine blessings and seaside relaxation.

Suggested 2 Days / 1 Night Itinerary

Day 1: Bhubaneswar ➔ Dhauli ➔ Raghurajpur ➔ Puri Beach

Morning pickup from Bhubaneswar Airport / Railway Station. En-route visit Dhauli Shanti Stupa and Raghurajpur heritage crafts village. Check-in at Puri hotel. Spend the evening at the certified Blue Flag Golden Beach with local seafood.

Day 2: Shree Jagannath Darshan ➔ Gundicha Temple ➔ Return

Early morning VIP Jagannath Temple darshan assistance and Mahaprasad (Ananda Bazar). Visit Gundicha Temple and Lokanath Temple. Afternoon scenic drive back to Bhubaneswar.
