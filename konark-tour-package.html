The Grand Chariot of the Sun God

Conceived as a colossal chariot with 24 carved stone wheels pulled by seven horses, the Konark Sun Temple is an architectural gem of ancient India. With Biswa's Tour and Travels, you get a comfortable, stress-free day tour originating from Bhubaneswar, featuring the best route via Pipli craft village and the scenic Puri-Konark Marine Drive.

Key Attractions Covered in this Tour

1. UNESCO Konark Sun Temple & ASI Museum

Explore the Natya Mandap, monumental stone wheels functioning as precise sundials, and the nearby Archaeological Survey of India museum preserving antique sculptures.

2. Chandrabhaga Beach

One of Asia's first eco-certified Blue Flag beaches, celebrated for its pristine waters, lighthouse views, and serene sunset walks.

3. Pipli Appliqué Craft Village & Ramachandi Beach

Shop for traditional Odisha patchwork lanterns and parasols in Pipli, and stop at the Kushabhadra river estuary at Ramachandi for water sports.
